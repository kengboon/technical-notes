# From ViT to CNN: An Experimental Note on DINOv3 Distillation

__Author:__ [Teo Keng Boon](https://github.com/kengboon)

__Keywords:__ _knowledge distillation, ViT, DINOv3, teacher-student_

## Abstract

Knowledge distillation (KD) involves a technique that transfer the learning of a more powerful "teacher" model to a smaller "student" model, the particular methodology also known as "teacher-student learning". The motivation is to cut down latency and computation cost, while maintaining the robust performance of the more powerful model. In the field of deep learning in computer vision, the vision transformers (ViTs) emerged as powerful alternatives of convolutional neural networks (CNNs).
